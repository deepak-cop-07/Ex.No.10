# Ex.No.10
Content Creation (Reports, Articles, Case Studies, etc.) Using Prompt Patterns

## Date:06-09-2026
## Reg. No.212223060039

## Aim:
To demonstrate how various prompting techniques (query decomposition, decision-making, semantic filtering, etc.) can be employed to create content such as reports, articles, case studies, or creative works like comic books, using ChatGPT or similar models. The objective is to highlight how different prompt structures affect the content's quality, coherence, and structure.

## Procedure:
1.	Introduction to Prompt Patterns: Begin by understanding the following prompt patterns:
○	Query Decomposition: Breaking down complex queries into smaller, actionable parts.
○	Decision Making: Asking the model to choose between options or directions.
○	Answer Engineering: Refining outputs by giving detailed instructions on how to structure or format the answer.
○	Fact Check List: Ensuring the content is factually accurate, especially for reports or case studies.
○	Tail Generation: Extending the content logically to create depth in storytelling or analysis.
○	Menu Actions: Presenting multiple action choices and guiding the AI to select one.
○	Semantic Filter: Applying filters to control the tone, style, and accuracy of the content.
2.	Choosing the Content Type: Decide on the type of content you want to create. This could be:
○	Reports (e.g., industry analysis, sustainability reports, etc.)
○	Case Studies (e.g., business solutions, technological innovations)
○	Articles (e.g., opinion pieces, educational articles)
○	Creative Content (e.g., comic book story, short stories, video scripts)
3.	For example, you could choose a business report on market trends, a case study on a successful startup, or even a story-based prompt like creating a fictional world for a comic.
4.	Creating the Prompts:
○	Start with simple prompts to generate initial content.
○	Gradually refine the prompts, moving toward more complex techniques like decision-making (asking the model to pick one of several options), tail generation (extending the narrative), and semantic filtering (adjusting style or tone).
5.	Generating and Refining Outputs: Use the model to generate initial drafts, and then refine the outputs using iterative adjustments:
○	For a report, prompt the model with basic data, then ask for a deeper analysis and insights.
○	For creative content, refine the initial story idea with specific details on characters, settings, or actions.
6.	Review and Evaluation: After generating content, evaluate the outputs for:
○	Coherence: Is the generated content logical and structured?
○	Creativity/Originality: Is the content engaging and fresh?
○	Accuracy: Is the content factually accurate (for reports and case studies)?
○	Tone and Style: Does the content match the intended tone (formal, creative, educational)?

# ENGINEERING DOMAIN

Artificial Intelligence

# PROJECT TITLE

AI Research Assistant Using Prompt Engineering

# PROBLEM STATEMENT

Engineering students often spend considerable time searching for technical information, understanding complex topics, preparing reports, and creating seminar presentations. An AI Research Assistant can help students organize information, generate content, summarize topics, and prepare structured reports using suitable prompts.

# OBJECTIVE

To develop a prompt-based AI Research Assistant that can:

Generate technical content.
Break complex topics into smaller sections.
Select suitable topics for a seminar.
Create structured reports.
Check generated information.
Control the tone and style of the content.
Improve content through prompt iteration.

# PROMPT DESIGN

Different prompt patterns were applied to the AI Research Assistant.

1. Basic Prompt

Prompt:

Explain Artificial Intelligence in healthcare.

Purpose:
To generate an initial response about the selected topic.

Expected Output:
The AI provides a general explanation of Artificial Intelligence and its applications in healthcare.

2. Query Decomposition

Prompt:

Explain Artificial Intelligence in healthcare by dividing the topic into the following sections:

Introduction
Applications
Benefits
Challenges
Future Scope
Conclusion

Explain each section in simple English for engineering students.

Purpose:
To break a complex topic into smaller and manageable sections.

Output:
The AI generates a properly organized explanation with separate sections.

3. Decision Making

Prompt:

I am preparing an engineering seminar. Choose the most suitable topic from the following:

A. AI in Healthcare
B. AI in Robotics
C. AI in Education
D. AI in Agriculture

Select the topic that is easiest to explain in a 10-minute seminar and give a reason for your choice.

Purpose:
To make the AI select the most appropriate option.

Output:
The AI selects AI in Healthcare because it has simple concepts, practical applications, benefits, challenges, and future possibilities that can be explained within a short seminar.

4. Answer Engineering

Prompt:

Prepare a structured report on "Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare".

Include:

Introduction
Problem
Applications
Benefits
Challenges
Ethical Considerations
Future Scope
Conclusion

Use simple English, headings, bullet points, and short paragraphs. Make the content suitable for engineering students. Avoid unnecessary technical terms and unsupported claims.

Purpose:
To control the structure, format, language, and quality of the AI response.

5. Fact Check List

Prompt:

Review the generated report on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare. Create a fact-check list and identify:

Information that is reliable.
Claims that require verification.
Exaggerated claims.
Information that should be removed.

Do not invent facts or sources.

Purpose:
To improve the accuracy and reliability of the generated content.

Output:
The AI identifies statements that need verification and removes exaggerated claims such as "AI can completely replace doctors."

6. Tail Generation

Prompt:

Continue the Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare report by adding:

Three future applications.
Two major challenges.
Possible solutions.
A short conclusion.

Purpose:
To extend the existing content logically and provide additional depth.

7. Semantic Filter

Prompt:

Rewrite the report on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare using the following filters:

Audience: Engineering students
Language: Simple English
Tone: Professional and educational
Style: Clear and structured
Avoid: Unnecessary jargon, exaggerated claims, and unsupported statistics.

Purpose:
To control the tone, style, language, and target audience of the generated content.

# PROMPT ITERATION

Prompt iteration means improving the prompt step by step to obtain a better output.

Iteration 1 – Basic Prompt

Explain AI in healthcare.

Output:
A general explanation with limited structure.

Iteration 2 – Specific Prompt

Explain AI in healthcare for engineering students. Include applications, benefits, challenges, and future scope.

Output:
The response becomes more relevant and organized.

Iteration 3 – Refined Prompt

Act as an AI Research Assistant for engineering students. Prepare a structured report on "Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare" with sections for introduction, applications, benefits, challenges, ethical considerations, future scope, and conclusion. Use simple English and avoid unsupported claims. Clearly distinguish current applications from future possibilities.

Output:
The response becomes more detailed, structured, relevant, and suitable for a seminar.

Iteration Result

Basic Prompt → Specific Prompt → Structured Prompt → Refined Prompt

The quality of the AI-generated content improves as more instructions are added to the prompt.

# ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS

The following ethical considerations were considered while developing the AI Research Assistant:

Privacy: Confidential or personal information should not be entered into the AI system.
Accuracy: AI-generated information should be verified before using it in academic or professional work.
Bias: AI responses may contain biases present in its training data.
Plagiarism: AI-generated content should be reviewed and appropriately used rather than submitted as completely original work.
Human Supervision: AI should assist students and researchers rather than completely replace human judgment.
False Information: Unsupported or uncertain information should not be presented as fact.
